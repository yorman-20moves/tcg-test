Fix faction identity in the rail; make the Lore Packet explain and enforce itself

The rail said "No faction assigned" for every card. It was scraping
docs/rulebook/03-factions.md for faction identity -- and that document became
generated two commits ago, which changed the heading format the parser depended
on. The fix is to delete the parser: data/factions.yaml is the source of truth,
and data should never be recovered from a rendering of itself.

The rail now reads the YAML directly and also shows the faction's window profile,
which is the map that computes Reach on the card you are looking at.

Lore Packet, which was seven unlabelled boxes:
- Each field now explains what goes in it and shows an example.
- Live context beside the fields that need it: the faction's printed weakness
  next to The Fall, the crew's plan next to Why This Crew, the level-up condition
  next to The Ascension.
- A completeness meter, and REQUIRED markers on True Detail and The Fall.
- A line at the top saying what the packet actually is: working notes, not printed
  text. You write the card's flavour sentence FROM it.

Enforcement, so the packet is used rather than merely captured:
- W9 rewritten to report one finding per card listing every missing required
  field, rather than one finding per field. Four warnings about the same card is
  noise, and noise teaches you to ignore the linter.
- W11 added: a card whose rules text is pure mechanism with no flavour sentence.
  First attempt used a keyword list and flagged "he draws all the squad's heat"
  and "feeds on the heavy energy of a wild room" -- both flavour. Rewritten to key
  off this game's own convention: rules terms are capitalised, prose is not.

W11 then found a real defect. Eight cards had a sentence glued to the next one
with no space after the full stop -- "a wild room.Tasha gains" -- which made the
flavour unreadable as prose. Fixed in all eight.

README gains a section answering what the Lore Packet is, what goes in each of
the seven boxes, and everywhere it gets used.
