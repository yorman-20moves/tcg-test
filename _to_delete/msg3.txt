Add the Library: every keyword, effect and status in one editable place

Having to remember what each keyword does was friction in the middle of designing,
and the same friction applies to the 51 effects and the statuses -- so this is one
Library rather than a keywords screen.

Each row shows the point cost, the full definition, whether it is unique to a
faction or shared, and how many cards use it. Entries nothing uses are tagged
unused, which is usually either a gap or a dead entry. The filter searches
definitions as well as names, so "steal" finds everything about stealing even
where the word is not in the name.

Full editing: create, edit, delete, rename. While setting a point cost the editor
shows every entry priced within a point of it, so a new entry can be costed
against its neighbours rather than guessed. Delete is guarded -- if cards still
use it you get the list and have to confirm. Rename goes through the same engine
as tools/rename.py and updates every card, data file and document at once;
editing the name field alone would orphan every card pointing at the old name.

The friction was worst inside the card editor, so that is fixed too:
- every effect in the picker now shows its definition inline instead of in a
  tooltip, and the picker is taller to suit
- every keyword available to the card's faction lists its definition beneath the
  chips, with the selected ones highlighted

New rule F10: a keyword carries a faction field in keywords.yaml AND appears in a
faction's unique list in factions.yaml. Those two can disagree, and if they do the
toolkit check silently reads the wrong one. Currently clean.
