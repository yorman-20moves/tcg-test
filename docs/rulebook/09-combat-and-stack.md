# 9. The Combat Wave & The Stack

Combat is decisive and can be initiated **once per Round per player**. A Combat Wave is a series
of individual 1-on-1 clashes that all resolve **simultaneously**.

---

## Step 1 — Declaration & Target Restriction

The attacking player uses their action to declare a Combat Wave.

- Pay **1 Energy for each unit** pushed forward to attack.
- The default attack type is **Physical Attack (PA)**.
- You must assign a target for each attacker.

**Target rule:** you **cannot** target Life Points directly if your opponent has any Characters
on the Board, unless the attacking Character has a stealth-based ability (see **Prowler**).

## Step 2 — The Reaction Window

The defending player takes their alternating action to respond. They may:

- Use a Character with **Protector** to intercept.
- Play a **Combat Tactic** from Hand.
- Activate a **Fast Ability**.

Only cards and abilities explicitly labelled **Combat Tactic** or **[Fast Ability]** operate at
this speed. Standard abilities can only be used during your normal Phase 2 action.

## Step 3 — The Stack (Last In, First Out)

If multiple Combat Tactics or Fast Abilities are triggered, they resolve in **reverse order**.
The last card played resolves first.

## Step 4 — Simultaneous Resolution

Combat damage is dealt simultaneously across all individual 1-on-1 clashes. Both the attacking
and the defending Character deal their default **PA** damage to each other's **PH** at the same
time.

*Executioner mechanics and other damage replacements occur during this step, bypassing the
standard math.*

**Complex Protector resolution:** if a Protector is directly attacked and uses its ability to
intercept a *different* attacker, it must conduct **both** combats. The intercepted (protected)
combat resolves first, followed immediately by the direct attack.

**Death:** any Character whose PH or MH drops to 0 is **Murdered**. Unblocked damage reduces
Life Points directly.
