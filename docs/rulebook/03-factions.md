# 3. The Factions (Lore & Core Identity)

Cards belong to one of four colored factions, representing different **methodologies** within
the crew.

> **Two-axis identity.** Faction is *how you win*. **Crew** (Latin Kings, Mobb 134, Shea's,
> Pimp Juice, PWNED, Fury Park) is *who you run with*. They are independent — one crew's
> members can span three factions. This is the game's best structural idea and both rubrics
> treat it as load-bearing. See [`../rubrics/lore-rubric.md`](../rubrics/lore-rubric.md).

Each faction below ends with its **printed weakness** — the rhetorical question that closes
its description. That question is a design contract: every faction must be able to lose to it.

---

## 🟥 Red — The Warmongers

To the Warmongers, the world is forged in violence and paid for in blood. They don't just
fight; they conquer. Their bodies are meticulously honed to be ultimate weapons and unbreakable
shields.

**Offense:** they hit with the force of a freight train, overwhelming blockers and spilling raw
physical destruction directly into the enemy line.

**Defense:** masochistic guardians, throwing their frames into the line of fire to absorb blows
meant for their crew, turning pain into permanent power.

They don't scheme or play mind games — they endure, they adapt, and they break you through
sheer physical dominance.

> **The printed weakness:** *But when a problem cannot be solved with fists, and their
> unbreakable bodies finally begin to fail, what will they have left?*

---

## 🟦 Blue — The Overthinkers

The Overthinkers view the battlefield as a chessboard, and everyone else is a pawn waiting to
be sacrificed. Cold, calculating intellect; physical brawling is the primitive flailing of
animals.

**Offense:** they bypass the body entirely, attacking your psyche and shattering your mental
resolve from afar.

**Defense:** they manipulate the flow of combat — forcing you to attack decoys, redirecting
strikes, laying traps that punish your aggression.

They hoard information, strip your hand of options, control the board, and dictate exactly how
and when you are allowed to breathe.

> **The printed weakness:** *But when their perfect plans are suddenly punched in the mouth,
> and the chaotic violence of the streets overwhelms their grand designs, how long can a genius
> survive a street fight?*

---

## 🟩 Green — The Assholes / Pwners

Why play fair when playing dirty pays so much better? The Assholes don't care about honor,
endurance, or intellectual supremacy — they care about winning by making you miserable. The
ultimate opportunists.

**Offense:** they bleed you dry, striking from the shadows where you can't hit back, extorting
your cards and resources.

**Defense:** they don't block. They evade, slip past your defenses, or plant spies directly
onto your side of the board to sabotage you from the inside.

They weaponize frustration — stealing your energy, hijacking your tempo, laughing while your
empire crumbles.

> **The printed weakness:** *But if they are ever dragged out of the shadows and forced into a
> fair, head-on collision, do they have the spine to survive it?*

---

## 🟨 Gold — The Icons

The Icons do not fight for survival; they fight for the soul of the city. Pillars of the
community, commanding absolute respect, ruling through a heavy aura of guilt and loyalty.

**Offense:** they don't need to throw a punch — they win the cultural war, accumulating so much
societal **Influence** that you simply cease to matter.

**Defense:** supreme caretakers. Healing the broken, pacifying violence before a punch is
thrown, crippling attackers by making them carry the shame of striking a legend.

Martyrs, sponsors, untouchable kings.

> **The printed weakness:** *But if their followers are slaughtered, and their pristine image is
> violently dragged through the mud, can a symbol actually bleed?*

> **Open question (OQ-06):** The Icons are the least-built faction by a wide margin. All five
> Icons cards are nameless stubs with no cost, stats, or text, no crew is assigned to any of
> them, and their win condition (Influence) has no printed support anywhere in the pool.
