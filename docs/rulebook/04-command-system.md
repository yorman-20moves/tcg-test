# 4. The Command System (Casting)

When you play a **Tactic**, an **Attachment**, or an **Arena** from your Hand, you are not
casting it yourself. You are the player giving the order — a Character on the Board must
execute it.

## The Rule

To play these cards you must have at least one Character of the **matching Faction color**
alive on your Board, and you must explicitly declare them as the **caster**.

## Strategic Interruption

If your opponent Murders your caster in response before the card resolves, your card fails
and goes to the Graveyard with no effect.

This can only be done via **Fast Abilities** or **Combat Tactics** — a player cannot initiate
a full Combat Wave as a reaction.

**Consequence:** board presence is existential. A wiped board means you cannot cast Tactics
to save yourself.
