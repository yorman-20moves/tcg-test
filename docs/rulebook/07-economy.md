# 7. The Economy: The Investment Engine

There are no dedicated "Energy" cards. **Any card in your Hand can become fuel.**

## Investments

During **Phase 1**, you may place cards face-down in your **Resource Row**.

## Energy Capacity

Your available Energy for the Round equals the number of face-down cards in your Resource Row,
plus any temporary Energy from card effects.

## Used and Unused Energy

- **Vertical** face-down card = **Unused Energy**.
- To pay a cost, turn the card **horizontal** = **Used Energy**.
- During Phase 1 (The Refresh), turn all Used cards back to Unused.

## The Reset

Energy capacity does not stack across Rounds. Five face-down cards means a capacity of 5
Energy to spend that Round — not five more than last Round.

## Why There Are No Mulligans

A hand of unplayable, high-cost cards is perfect fuel to permanently ramp your Resource Row.
The bad hand is the ramp.

**The tension:** committing 3 cards to your Resource Row on Round 1 gives massive Energy and
leaves you almost nothing to play. Your hand is your only fuel source. Ramp wisely.
