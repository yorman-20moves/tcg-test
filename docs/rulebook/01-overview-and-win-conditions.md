# 1. Game Overview & Win Conditions

A 1-on-1 trading card game where you command different versions of your friends across
distinct factions. Players alternate single actions to outmaneuver each other, manage a
shared Round structure, and fight for dominance across physical, mental, and social
battlegrounds.

## Match Structure

- **Match:** best 2 out of 3 **Games**, unless both players agree to another format before playing.
- **Game:** a full sequence of **Rounds** played until a Win Condition is met. At the start of
  every Game, Life Points reset and decks are reshuffled.
- **Starting Life Points:** 30, per Game.

## How to Win (The Three Victories)

### The Beatdown — physical / mental

Reduce your opponent's Life Points to 0. Both unblocked **Physical Attack (PA)** and
**Mental Attack (MA)** deal direct damage to Life Points.

### The Influence Threshold — spiritual

Accumulate 30 **Influence Points** through Character abilities or Tactic cards to achieve a
social/cultural victory.

> **Open question (OQ-02):** Influence is a printed win condition with almost no printed
> support. Exactly one keyword (Martyr) references it, and no card in the pool currently
> grants a defined amount. See [open-questions.md](../design/open-questions.md).

### Concession — the surrender

If you create an overwhelming board state, or your opponent runs out of viable options,
they may concede at any time.

> **Open question (OQ-03):** Concession is a social act, not a win condition. Listing it as
> one hides the fact that the game has no deck-out loss and therefore no natural clock.
