# 13. Glossary of Universal Terms

**Countering** — if an ability or card states that it *Counters* an opponent's card, action, or
Combat Wave, it completely invalidates it. The countered card is sent to the Graveyard and none
of its effects resolve. If a Combat Wave is countered, all attacks within the wave are negated
and the wave immediately ends.

**Murdered** — a Character whose PH or MH has reached 0 through active damage or a card effect.
Murdered Characters go to their **original owner's** Graveyard.

**Caster** — the Character you declare as executing a Tactic, Attachment, or Arena. Must match
the card's faction color and be alive on your Board. See [Command System](04-command-system.md).

**Locked** — the state both players reach in the Blind Commit once each has chosen their
face-down Investment cards, before simultaneous reveal.

**Permanent damage** — damage to PH or MH. Persists across Rounds until explicitly healed.
All PH/MH damage is permanent; there is no "damage clears at end of turn."

**Temporary** — expires during Phase 3, the End Step. Applies to buffs, debuffs, and Energy.

**Influence Points** — the currency of the third win condition. 30 wins the Game.

**Initiative Token** — the physical marker determining who acts first in Phase 2. Passes to the
other player at the end of every Round.

**Reaction Window** — Step 2 of a Combat Wave, the only window in which Combat Tactics and Fast
Abilities may be played.

## Terms used on cards that are NOT yet defined here

These appear in card text or in the Elements point table but have no rulebook definition.
Each is a gate **G2** failure waiting to happen.

- **Sacrifice** — used in the Graveyard rules and in Infiltrator's text; never defined.
- **Hexproof / Untargetable** — appears in the effect table; not in the rulebook.
- **Spell Shield** — appears in the effect table; not in the rulebook.
- **Taxing** — appears in the effect table; not in the rulebook.
- **Recursion** — appears in the effect table; not in the rulebook.
- **Arena** — referenced in §4 and §5; has no rules of its own.
