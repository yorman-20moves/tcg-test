# 2. Deck Construction & Zones of Play

## The Total Card Pool

- **Minimum 40 cards.**
- Maximum **3 copies** of the exact same card.

## Ascendant Limits

- Maximum **5 Ascendants** in your pool.
- Up to 3 copies of the same Ascendant, but **each copy counts toward the 5-card VIP limit**.
- Running zero Ascendants is legal.

> **Open question (OQ-01):** Every card currently designed is an Ascendant. At a 5-Ascendant
> cap on a 40-card pool, ~88% of a legal deck is made of card types that do not exist yet.

## The Zones

| Zone | What lives there |
|---|---|
| **The Main Deck** | Your supply of available cards. |
| **The Hand** | Your available options. Start with **6 cards**. No maximum hand size. **No mulligans.** |
| **The Board** | Where Descendants and Ascendants fight. No limit on Characters. |
| **The Resource Row** | Face-down **Investment** cards that generate your Energy. |
| **The Tactic Zone** | Behind your Characters. Active Attachments and Arenas sit here face-up. |
| **The Graveyard** | A single discard pile for used Tactics, destroyed Attachments, Sacrificed cards, and Murdered Characters. |
| **The VIP Area** | Your Ascendants, face-up, from the start of the Game. They are never shuffled into the Main Deck. |

**Ownership rule:** cards always go to their **original owner's** Graveyard, regardless of who
controlled them when they were destroyed or sacrificed.
