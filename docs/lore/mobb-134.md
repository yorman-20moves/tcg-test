## 4. The Code — **CG3**

**The rule they do not break:** Nothing happens until it's written.

**What happens to someone who breaks it:** They're not punished — they're *unwritten*. The
incident is struck from the book, which means it never happened, which means nobody in the crew
owes you anything for it. You keep the bruises and lose the credit. *[INVENTED]*

**The rule they all quietly break:** Every one of them has done something they made sure never
got entered. The Bible is complete and all five of them know it isn't. *[INVENTED]*

**Who is closest to getting caught:** ⚡ Julio. His whole method is printed: Paralyzing Presence
and the Ooze Gauntlet only work on Characters with *lower* Physical Attack than his **[CARDS]**.
He has never once used the Commandments on someone who could hit back, and the ledger shows it
if anyone reads down the column instead of across. *[INVENTED]*

## 6. The Fracture ⚡

**The disagreement that hasn't been settled:** Dre's Commandments were supposed to end the part
where the strongest man on the block decides what's fair. But the Commandments are enforced by
Julio, who — printed, on the card — can only touch people weaker than he is. Benny has said the
quiet version once: the book didn't replace the old rule, it gave it a filing system.

**Dre's position and why he's right:** A law nobody enforces is a suggestion. Julio is the
reason the block obeys, and the block obeying is the only thing that's ever worked. Purity is a
luxury of people who don't have to hold territory.

**Benny's position and why he's also right:** Benny is the one who intercepts the hit meant for
somebody else **[CARDS]** — he sees exactly who ends up on the receiving end of the
Commandments, and it is never anybody dangerous. He's not arguing morality. He's arguing that
they've built a machine that only works downhill, and one day it'll meet something it can't
punch down at.

**Why it hasn't come to a head yet:** Because it works. Nothing has tested it.

**What would force it:** A Commandment that has to be enforced against someone stronger than
Julio. **This is a card.** *[INVENTED — and the most direct design hook in this document.]*
