# Canon

The world's fixed facts. **Check this before writing any lore; contradicting it fails lore gate
L3.** Everything here is currently unwritten, and that is the finding.

---

## The Game

**Name:** **Known Associates** — decided 2026-08-06, [D-014](decisions.md).

**Founding edition:** **Known Associates: Local Legends.**

**The name is used flat.** Applying police-file language to a friend group is the joke. No card,
no flavour sentence and no rulebook line may *acknowledge* that it is one — see **Register**,
below. This is the easiest place in the project to break that rule by accident.

**It deliberately does not cover the Icons.** A mother is kin, not an associate. `factions.yaml`
already marks them `exempt: true`, outside the system by design, and the name excluding them
hardens it: everybody else is on the list, your mother isn't on anybody's.

## The Factions

The four methodologies. Full text: [`../rulebook/03-factions.md`](../rulebook/03-factions.md).

| Faction | Methodology | Printed weakness — **must be able to happen** |
|---|---|---|
| 🟥 Warmongers | Win by physical dominance | When fists don't solve it, what's left? |
| 🟦 Overthinkers | Win by out-thinking | How long does a genius survive a street fight? |
| 🟩 Assholes | Win by making you miserable | Dragged into a fair fight, do they have the spine? |
| 🟨 Icons | Win by cultural weight | If the image is dragged through the mud, can a symbol bleed? |

**WG5 status:** none of the four weaknesses has happened on the record to anybody. Each needs at
least one card or event documenting it.
