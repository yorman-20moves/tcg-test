# Julito, Harbinger of the Demon

**Warmongers** · Pwners · Ascendant

## Base Side

`Cost 4` — **PA 4 / PH 4 / MA 1 / MH 3**

Keywords: Combo-Striker

### Rules Text

[Build Meter] (Trigger Ability): Julito lives for a bloody, unbroken combo.
Every time Julito Murders an enemy Character or lands a successful attack on the opponent's Life Points, he gains 1 EX Meter token (Maximum 3).

[The EX Enhancements] (Passive Ability): 

   Level 1: +2 PA and +2 PH. 

   Level 2: Gains (Combo-Striker) keyword. (His combat resolves in two strikes: a free first strike without retaliation, then a simultaneous strike).

## Level-Up

### Condition

Julito loses his mind the second his meter is full.
When Julito successfully reaches 3 EX Meter tokens, he levels up.

Keywords gained: Berserker

### Ascended Rules Text

[Demonic Ascension] (Passive Ability):  Julio is drunk with the power of the dark Hadou. As long as Julito has 3 EX Meter tokens, he gains the (Berserker) keyword. (He gains +1 Physical Attack (PA) every time he takes permanent Physical Health (PH) damage).

[Shun Goku Satsu] (Fast Ability): Julito delivers a thousand fatal strikes in the blink of an eye. Pay 1 Energy, spend all 3 EX Meter tokens, and immediately Murder a target enemy Character. After this resolves, flip Julito back to his base form.
