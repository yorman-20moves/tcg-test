# Lenny, the Kawaii Connoisseur

**Overthinkers** · Pwners · Ascendant

## Base Side

`Cost 3` — **PA 2 / PH 3 / MA 3 / MH 5**

### Rules Text

[Kawaii Glow-Up] (Fast Ability): Lenny hands out a magical makeover that heals wounds.
Pay 1 Energy to instantly restore 2 Physical Health (PH) or Mental Health (MH) to a target allied Character.

[Kawaii Summon] (Trigger Ability): When summoned, search your deck for a Kawaii Guardian and summon it.

## Level-Up

### Condition

Lenny ascends, overwhelmed by his ultimate cute creation.
If Maji Kawaii Megami is on the Board, Lenny levels up.

### Ascended Rules Text

[Kawaii Immunity] (Passive Ability): Lenny changes the rules so that nothing ugly is allowed.
All allied Characters are completely immune to negative status effects.

[Optimal Pathing] (Passive Ability): Allied Pwner Characters have the Energy cost of their abilities reduced by 1.
