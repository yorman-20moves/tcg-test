# Ruvi, Player One

**Overthinkers** · Pwners · Ascendant

## Base Side

`Cost 5` — **PA 1 / PH 3 / MA 5 / MH 4**

### Rules Text

[The Item Box] (Fast Ability): Ruvi is bored and wants to see if pure RNG can carry him. Pay 1 Energy and roll a standard 6-sided die (d6). Apply the following effect immediately:

1-2 (Banana Peel): Bad luck. Ruvi takes 1 permanent Mental Health (MH) damage.

3-4 (Green Shell): Ruvi deals 2 Physical Attack (PA) damage to a target enemy Character.

5 (Mushroom): Ruvi permanently gains +2 Physical Health (PH) and +2 Mental Health (MH).

6 (The Blue Shell): Ruvi instantly deals 4 Mental Attack (MA) damage to a target enemy Character.

## Level-Up

### Condition

Ruvi hits the jackpot and realizes the world is a game.
When Ruvi successfully rolls a 6 on [The Item Box], he levels up.

### Ascended Rules Text

[You Know This Is Just A Game, Right?] : Ruvi has realized that he is just a card and uses a GameShark to hack the system. As long as Ruvi is face-up on the Board, all other Characters (allied and enemy) are Silenced.

[The Glitch]: When Ruvi declares a Combat Wave, you may look the opponent in the eye and say, "It's just a game." If you do, Ruvi deals his Physical Attack (PA) damage directly to your opponent's Life Points immediately upon declaration, completely bypassing the Reaction Window and simultaneous combat math.
