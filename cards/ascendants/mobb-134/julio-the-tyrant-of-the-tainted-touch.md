# Julio, The Tyrant of the Tainted Touch

**Warmongers** · Mobb 134 · Ascendant

## Base Side

`Cost 5` — **PA 4 / PH 4 / MA 4 / MH 3**

### Rules Text

[Paralyzing Presence] (Trigger Ability): Julio freezes his victims in pure panic just by walking into the room.
When summoned to the Board, inflict Stun on one enemy Character with a lower Physical Attack (PA) than Julio until the end of the Round.

[The Ooze Gauntlet] (Fast Ability): Pay 1 Energy to inflict Fear on an enemy Character that has a lower PA than him until the end of the Round.

## Level-Up

### Condition

Julio smells their fear and stops playing nice.
When Julio has seen 3 or more enemy Characters have been afflicted by Stun or Fear, Julio levels up.

### Ascended Rules Text

[The Meek Executioner] (Executioner): Julio just walks up and snaps their frozen necks.
