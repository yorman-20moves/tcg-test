# Moammar, The One Punch Machine Gun

**Warmongers** · Mobb 134 · Ascendant

## Base Side

`Cost 5` — **PA 6 / PH 6 / MA 0 / MH 3**

### Rules Text

[Heart of the Cards] (Fast Ability): Moammar is crazy, but he always has a plan.
Pay 2 Energy to search your Main Deck for a Warmonger Tactic card, reveal it, place it in your Hand, and shuffle your deck.

## Level-Up

### Condition

Moammar gets ready to swing the moment the board is clear.
The exact moment the opponent has zero cards on their Board (Characters, Tactics, or Attachments), Moammar levels up.

### Ascended Rules Text

[The One-Punch] (Trigger Ability): Moammar throws one punch with all his power, not caring if he dies.
He can only declare an attack once per Game. When he attacks, permanently increase his Physical Attack (PA) by the opponent's current Life Points. During Phase 3 (The End Step), the recoil Murders Moammar.
