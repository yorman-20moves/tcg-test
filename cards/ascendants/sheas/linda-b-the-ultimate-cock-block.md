# Linda B, The Ultimate Cock-Block

**Assholes** · Shea's · Ascendant

## Base Side

`Cost 5` — **PA 3 / PH 4 / MA 3 / MH 4**

### Rules Text

[Not Tonight, Boyo] (Fast Ability): Linda blocks their nonsense with pure Irish spite.
Pay 1 Energy during the Reaction Window to Counter an opponent's Standard or Combat Tactic.

## Level-Up

### Condition

Linda gets tired of the games and decides to end the party.
When there are 6 or more Tactic cards in the opponent's Graveyard, Linda levels up.

### Ascended Rules Text

[Lockdown] (Fast Ability): Linda drops the shields right on their heads, crushing them.
Once per Round, pay 1 Energy to drop inescapable hard-light barriers on up to two target enemy Characters, inflicting Stunned until the end of the Round.
