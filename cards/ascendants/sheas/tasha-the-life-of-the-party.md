# Tasha, The Life of the Party

**Warmongers** · Shea's · Ascendant

## Base Side

`Cost 3` — **PA 2 / PH 3 / MA 1 / MH 1**

### Rules Text

[Pre-Game Buzz] (Passive Ability): Tasha feeds on the heavy energy of a wild room.
Tasha gains +1 Physical Attack (PA) for every Character on the Board equipped with a Beer or Blunt Attachment, or possessing permanent MH damage from a "Drink" ability.

[Shots! Shots! Shots!] (Fast Ability): Pay 1 Energy to equip a Beer Attachment from your Hand to Tasha or an ally.

## Level-Up

### Condition

Tasha peaks when surrounded by trashed fighters.
When Tasha sees 4 or more "intoxicated" Characters on the Board, she levels up.

Keywords gained: Combo-Striker

### Ascended Rules Text

[Liquid Courage] (Passive Ability): Tasha becomes a total wrecking ball, drunk and happy.
Tasha gains the Combo-Striker keyword. (Her combat resolves in two strikes: a free first strike without retaliation, then a simultaneous strike). Additionally, her Physical Health (PH) cannot drop below 1 as long as she is intoxicated.
