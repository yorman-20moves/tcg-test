# Jean H, The Irish Exit

**Overthinkers** · Pimp Juice · Ascendant

## Base Side

`Cost 4` — **PA 3 / PH 3 / MA 3 / MH 3**

### Rules Text

[Ghosting] (Fast Ability): e draws all the squad's heat, then effortlessly vanishes out the back.
Pay 1 Energy at any time to completely remove Jean H from the Board. He returns to your side of the Board at the start of the next Round's Phase 1 (The Refresh).

## Level-Up

### Condition

He isn't building an empire; he’s just chasing the ultimate high.
When Jean H has at least 3 Blunt or 3 Beer Attachments equipped to him at the same time, he levels up.

### Ascended Rules Text

[Untouchable] (Passive Ability): When fully in the zone, fatal consequences just slip right past Jean.
While Jean H has at least one Beer or Blunt Attachment equipped to him, his Physical Health (PH) and Mental Health (MH) cannot be reduced below 1.
