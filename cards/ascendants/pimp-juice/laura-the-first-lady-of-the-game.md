# Laura, The First Lady of The Game

**Overthinkers** · Pimp Juice · Ascendant

## Base Side

`Cost 5` — **PA 1 / PH 3 / MA 5 / MH 5**

### Rules Text

[Fatal Attraction] (Fast Ability): Laura breaks their minds, making them take bullets for her.
Pay 2 Energy to target an enemy Character. Until the end of the Round, any damage that would be dealt to Laura is redirected and dealt to that hypnotized enemy Character instead.

[Sweet Talk] (Fast Ability): Pay 1 Energy to grant any Character +4 Mental Health (MH) until the end of the Round.

## Level-Up

### Condition

Laura takes total control now that her empire is built.
When Laura has watched other Characters tank a cumulative total of 10 or more damage in her stead, she levels up.

### Ascended Rules Text

[The Madame] (Passive Ability): Laura ensures her slaves don't just protect her, they do it in terror.
[Fatal Attraction] now costs 0 Energy. Any enemy Character that takes damage on her behalf is immediately inflicted with Fear.
