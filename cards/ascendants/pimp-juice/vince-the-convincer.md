# Vince, The Convincer

**Overthinkers** · Pimp Juice · Ascendant

## Base Side

`Cost 5` — **PA 1 / PH 5 / MA 5 / MH 5**

### Rules Text

[The Suggestion] (Fast Ability): Vince tricks them into shooting their own friends.
During the Reaction Window, pay 2 Energy to redirect an attacker's declared attack to any other viable target. Realizing they've been played inflicts 1 Mental Attack (MA) damage to the attacker.

## Level-Up

### Condition

Vince takes the wheel, tired of just whispering.
When Vince successfully redirects 3 attacks, he levels up.

### Ascended Rules Text

[The Hijack] (Standard Ability): Vince completely hijacks their mind and makes them fight for him.
Once per Round, target an enemy Character with a Mental Health (MH) lower than Vince's Mental Attack (MA). Move them to your side of the Board to fight for you until Phase 3.
