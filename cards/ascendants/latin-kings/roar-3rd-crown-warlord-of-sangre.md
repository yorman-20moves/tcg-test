# RoaR, 3rd Crown - Warlord of Sangre

**Warmongers** · Latin Kings · Ascendant

## Base Side

`Cost 6` — **PA 8 / PH 6 / MA 4 / MH 6**

Keywords: Juggernaut

### Rules Text

[Point Out The Strongest One] (Trigger Ability): RoaR just wants to fight the biggest guy in the room.
When summoned, bypass the Combat Wave limit and immediately attack the enemy Character with the highest Physical Attack (PA).

[What’s a Handicap?] (Juggernaut): RoaR crushes anyone stupid enough to stand in his way. If RoaR's attack is defended, any Physical Attack (PA) damage that exceeds the defender’s remaining Physical Health (PH) spills over directly to the opponent’s Life Points.

## Level-Up

### Condition

RoaR finally finds a real fight when he faces down an army.
When the total combined Physical Attack (PA) of your opponent's Board is 10 or greater, RoaR levels up.

### Ascended Rules Text

[Did you know you can wield weapons with BOTH HANDS?] (Passive Ability): RoaR stops holding back and grabs his weapon with both hands.
His Physical Attack (PA) is permanently locked at 10, plus an additional amount equal to half of your opponent's remaining Life Points (rounded down).
